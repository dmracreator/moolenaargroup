Sectors & markets

The industries that move and feed the world.

Florex Group operates across a diversified portfolio — from global logistics and commodity trade to horticulture, life science, technology and the wood industry. These are the markets where our expertise and network create lasting value.

Supply chain

Logistics

Forwarding, warehousing and multimodal transport — deep sea, short sea, barge, rail, road and air — through a network of more than 150 offices worldwide.

Global trade

Commodities & Trade

Commodity trade and supply-chain finance, moving goods between continents and connecting producers with global markets.

Floriculture

Flowers, plants and floricultural products for professional and retail markets — the sector where the Florex story began in 1850.
