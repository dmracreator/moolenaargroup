About Florex Group

Uniting long heritage with a disciplined investment approach.

Since 1850 the Florex name has grown from Dutch floriculture into an international investment and business-development group — combining more than 170 years of trading experience with a clear, long-term view of value.

Our story

From Dutch floriculture to global markets.

Founded in 1850, Florex began in the flower fields of the Netherlands and steadily expanded into international trade, logistics and strategic investment. That heritage — privately held and independently run — still shapes how we work today.

Based in the Netherlands, the gateway to Europe, we operate across many industries and markets. Our team of experienced experts selects the right companies and strengthens their growth strategy, backed by a network of more than 150 offices worldwide.

Our approach

How we partner with the companies we back.
