Who we are

An independent investment group with a global outlook.

Moolenaar Group is a Dutch investment and business-development group with a legacy dating back to 1850. Based in the Netherlands, the gateway to Europe, we specialise in international trade, strategic investment and long-term partnerships that add value to our global portfolio.

Our experienced team selects the right companies across logistics, commodities, horticulture, life science and technology, then activates a worldwide network to strengthen their growth strategy.
