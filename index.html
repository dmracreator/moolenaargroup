Who we are

Uniting tradition with vision.

Florex Group is a Dutch family-owned business with a legacy dating back to 1850. Based in the Netherlands — the gateway to Europe — we specialise in international trade, strategic investment and business development, actively seeking long-term partnerships that add value to our global portfolio.

Operating across many industries and markets, our team of experienced experts in logistics, real estate development, international trade and agriculture is highly skilled at selecting the right companies and strengthening their growth strategy.
